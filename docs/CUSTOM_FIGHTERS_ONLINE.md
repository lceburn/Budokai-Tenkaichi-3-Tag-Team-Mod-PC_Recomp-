# Custom fighters in native rooms

Install the same experimental update on every Windows computer in the room.
Create and save your fighter in Z Warriors first.

1. Host or join your normal LAN or internet room.
2. Open Team Battle setup and select your fighter slot.
3. Use the standard fighter picker. Your saved customs appear at the end of the
   normal roster, with their linked forms in the usual form selector.
4. Confirm your fighter, costume and equipment. Wait for sharing and room
   verification to finish, then ready up.

The selected fighter and its linked transformations are shared automatically.
The room has space for nineteen custom forms total: each base fighter and each
transformation counts as one. A family that does not fit is rejected as a whole.

Downloaded fighters remain available for the room and rematches. They are not
added to your saved character library, and temporary room files are removed
when you leave. Your saved characters, settings and mods remain local.

To make a variant of your own saved fighter, select it in the Z Warriors library
and press Square to duplicate it. Edit that independent copy and link it as a
transformation if desired. The local saved-library limit remains four forms.

This is a networking test update. Localhost transfer, transformations, rematches,
multi-team matches, late joining and disconnect handling have been checked.
The next acceptance test is LAN and internet play on separate computers.
